# Mori Paper Linework Logo

## Direction

The active Mori logo now follows the soft paper-and-watercolor style shown in the latest app references. The mark is a generated raster forest landscape circle: layered moss-green canopy forms, a quiet seed-gold light, warm paper texture, and a thin deep-forest outline.

This is intentionally simpler than the sprout badge route. It keeps one focal symbol so the app icon remains legible at small iOS sizes.

## Preserve

- Exact brand spelling: `Mori`.
- Generated PNG source, not SVG/vector, as the visual master.
- Warm paper background `#F7F5F0` / `#FFF8EA`.
- Deep forest ink `#173D32`, moss `#687E5E`, and seed gold `#D8B86F`.
- Simple circular mark with soft layered forest/canopy forms.
- Separate light and reverse lockups for light and forest-dark backgrounds.

## Avoid

- Kanji, torii, bonsai, or literal Japanese decoration.
- Overt mortality symbols.
- Busy tree silhouettes, dense branch detail, or small generated text.
- Any SVG/vector file as the master logo source; SVG exports are not allowed to replace the generated PNG master.
